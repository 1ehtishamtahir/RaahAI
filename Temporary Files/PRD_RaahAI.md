# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# RaahAI

### Your Smart Guide to Government Services

Version: 1.0 Project Type: AI Government Assistant Hackathon: Bano Qabil
AI Hackathon 2026

## 1. Overview

RaahAI is an AI-powered Government Assistant designed to simplify access
to Pakistani government services. It enables citizens to ask questions
in Urdu or English, understand official procedures, upload government
documents for explanation, and receive personalized guidance backed by
official information.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many Pakistani citizens struggle with confusing government websites,
English-only instructions, difficult forms, and repeated office visits
due to missing documents.

## 3. Vision

Make government services understandable, accessible, and user-friendly
for every citizen through AI.

## 4. Mission

Provide an intelligent AI assistant that guides citizens through
government processes using trusted information, multilingual support,
and document understanding.

## 5. Core Features

-   AI Government Chat
-   Document Explainer
-   OCR Form Scanner
-   Voice Assistant
-   Urdu & English support
-   Personalized document checklist
-   RAG with official government sources

## 6. MVP Scope

Services: - Passport - CNIC - Business Registration

## 7. Tech Stack

Frontend: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS

Backend: FastAPI

Database: PostgreSQL

Vector DB: ChromaDB

AI: Alibaba Cloud Qwen + Embeddings

OCR: PaddleOCR / Tesseract

Speech: Whisper + TTS

Deployment: Vercel, Render, Supabase

## 8. Success Metrics

-   Fast responses
-   Accurate OCR
-   Trusted answers with citations
-   Successful end-to-end demo

## 9. Demo Flow

1.  Ask passport question.
2.  AI explains process.
3.  Upload form.
4.  OCR extracts fields.
5.  AI explains fields.
6.  Receive checklist.

## 10. Future

Driving License, Tax Filing, Office Locator, Appointment Booking,
Notifications.
